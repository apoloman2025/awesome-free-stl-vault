## ✨ Highlights & Features

- **Direct High-Speed Downloads:** Zero URL shorteners, scam redirects, or intrusive popups.
- **Pre-Supported Files:** Many miniature models include chitubox/lychee supported and sliced files ready for resin printing.
- **Studio Archive:** Daily updated releases from top tabletop sculptors and digital studios.
- **Completely Free:** No mandatory registration or subscription paywalls required to access models.

## ⚔️ Categories

### 🎲 Tabletop Wargaming & RPG Miniatures
High-detail proxy models, fantasy armies, monsters, sci-fi space troopers, and DnD miniatures:
- Grimdark Warriors & Sci-Fi Battle Infantry
- Fantasy Orcs, Elves, Paladins, and Dragons
- Eldritch Horrors, Undead Legions & Demons
- Modular Bases and Miniature Adapters

Explore full tabletop collection: **[Browse Miniatures on FreeSTLFiles](https://freestlfiles.online/)**

### 🦸 Statues, Busts & Anime Figurines
Collector-grade character sculptures with split parts and keys for easy painting:
- Anime, Manga & Video Game Characters
- Superhero & Comic Book Sculptures
- Anatomical and Expressive Busts
- Fanart Figure Sculptures

### 🏰 Modular Scenery, Dungeons & Terrains
Immersive battleground assets for miniature wargames and roleplaying campaigns:
- Modular Dungeon Walls, Tiles & Pillars
- Gothic Cathedrals, Ruins & Fortresses
- Sci-Fi Bunkers, Industrial Pipes & Crates
- Scatter Terrain (Barrels, Chests, Campfires)

### ⚙️ Functional & Calibration Models
Tools to dial in your 3D printer and everyday utility prints:
- Resin & FDM Exposure Calibration Cones / Torture Tests
- Filament Spool Adapters and Cable Chains
- Tabletop Paint Station Holders & Tool Trays

## 🛠️ Recommended 3D Printing Tools & Slicers

- **[OrcaSlicer](https://github.com/OrcaSlicer/OrcaSlicer):** All-in-one open-source FDM slicer with advanced calibration tests.
- **[PrusaSlicer](https://github.com/prusa3d/PrusaSlicer):** Feature-rich open-source slicer for FDM and SLA.
- **[Lychee Slicer](https://mango3d.io/):** Powerful slicer specifically designed for resin 3D printing and auto-supports.
- **[ChituBox](https://www.chitubox.com/):** Widely used resin 3D printing preparation tool.
- **[UVtools](https://github.com/sn4k3/UVtools):** Layer-by-layer SLA/DLP validator and repair tool for resin printers.
